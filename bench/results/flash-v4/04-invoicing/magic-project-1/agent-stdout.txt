- `aging({ today = todayISO() } = {}, store = getStore())` walks `store.invoices`, keeps only `status === "issued"`, computes `daysPastDue = daysBetween(invoice.due, today)` and adds `invoice.balance` to `current` (`<= 0`), `days1to30`, `days31to60`, `days61to90` or `over90`, then sets `total` to the sum of the five buckets.
