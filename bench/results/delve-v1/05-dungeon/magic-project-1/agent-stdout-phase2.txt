## Decisions I made where the brief was open

The full list is in the note and design §11; the load-bearing ones:

- `state.history` holds JSON **strings**, each the pre-action state with `history`/`undo` stripped, so snapshots can't nest and the state stays plainly serialisable.
- The snapshot is taken before the action and pushed only if it turned out to cost a turn — that is what makes "actions that cost no turn leave nothing to undo" fall out for free.
- Restoring skips `history`/`undo` in its key-deletion pass, so the state's key order survives and `serialize()` after a rewind is byte-identical to before the undone action (stronger than the brief's `hash()` requirement, and cheap).
- `undo()` works whatever the status: rewinding a death revives the player, rewinding after the amulet removes the win. The brief restricts `act`, not `undo`.
- The rewind messages are return values only — never appended to `state.log`, since the state must come back exactly as it was. The page shows the line in the `#log` panel as a trailing display-only line.
