The generator follows design section 5 exactly, which is what the population task depends on: one `rng.int(7)` for the room target (6..12), then four `rng.int` draws per placement attempt (`w`, `h`, `x`, `y` — drawn even when the attempt is rejected) until the target is reached or 4000 attempts are spent; a round yielding fewer than 6 rooms is discarded and redrawn with the generator continuing, up to 20 rounds, then a fixed 6-room fallback layout. Rooms are carved only after all placement draws, then one `rng.next()` per consecutive room pair chooses the corridor axis (`< 0.5` means horizontal first), carving `.` over `#` only. `start` is the floored centre of `rooms[0]`, and `stairs` is the floored centre of the non-first room with the farthest centre (squared Euclidean from `start`, first on a tie), marked `>` for depth < 5 and left as `.` for depth 5.
