Done. Here's what I changed:

**`src/todo.js`** — added the three methods:
- `toggle(id)` flips `done`, returns the item, and throws `new Error("Unknown id: ...")` when not found.
- `remove(id)` splices the item out and returns `true`, or `false` if unknown.
